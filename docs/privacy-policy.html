40 Kun

Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 20-may, 2026

40 Kun — 40 kunlik odat shakllantirishga yordam beruvchi offline-first mobil ilova. Ushbu siyosat ilova foydalanuvchi ma'lumotlariga qanday munosabatda bo'lishini tushuntiradi.

Qisqa mazmun

Ilova hisob yaratishni talab qilmaydi, reklama yoki analytics ishlatmaydi va odatlaringizni bizning serverlarga yubormaydi. Odat nomlari, sabablar, reja, check-in tarixi, eslatma sozlamalari va boshqa ilova ma'lumotlari qurilmangizda lokal saqlanadi.

Qanday ma'lumotlar saqlanadi

Ilova ishlashi uchun quyidagi ma'lumotlarni qurilmangizda saqlashi mumkin:

  • tanlangan asosiy odat va mikro odatlar;
  • odatga bog'langan emoji, shaxsiyat jumlasi, sabab va kundalik reja;
  • check-in tarixi, ketma-ketlik va milestone holatlari;
  • eslatma vaqti, snooze sozlamalari, mavzu va haptic sozlamalari;
  • ixtiyoriy yozilgan fikr-mulohazalar yoki qaydlar.

Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Ma'lumotlar qurilmangizning lokal xotirasida saqlanadi. 40 Kun hozircha foydalanuvchi akkaunti, cloud sync yoki tashqi ma'lumotlar bazasidan foydalanmaydi.

Bildirishnomalar

Agar ruxsat bersangiz, ilova odatlarni eslatish uchun lokal bildirishnomalardan foydalanadi. Bildirishnoma ruxsatini istalgan payt telefon sozlamalaridan o'chirishingiz mumkin.

Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Ilova Google Play, Expo va operatsion tizim xizmatlari orqali tarqatilishi yoki yangilanishi mumkin. Bu xizmatlar o'z texnik loglari va siyosatlariga ega bo'lishi mumkin. 40 Kun ilova ichida reklama tarmoqlari yoki tracking analytics qo'shmaydi.

Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Ilova ichidagi Sozlamalar bo'limida barcha lokal odatlar va tarixni tozalash mumkin. Ilovani qurilmadan o'chirish ham lokal ilova ma'lumotlarini olib tashlaydi.

Bolalar maxfiyligi

Ilova bolalardan ataylab shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'ish uchun mo'ljallanmagan. Agar ota-ona yoki vasiy bolaga oid ma'lumot noto'g'ri saqlangan deb hisoblasa, ilova ma'lumotlarini qurilmadan o'chirishi mumkin.

O'zgarishlar

Ushbu maxfiylik siyosati vaqti-vaqti bilan yangilanishi mumkin. Yangilangan versiyada sahifadagi sana o'zgartiriladi.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

40 Kun is an offline-first mobile app that helps users build a habit over a 40-day chilla journey. This policy explains how the app handles user information.

Summary

The app does not require an account, does not use ads or analytics, and does not send your habit data to our servers. Habit names, reasons, plans, check-in history, reminder settings, and other app data are stored locally on your device.

Information stored by the app

The app may store the following information on your device:

  • your selected main habit and optional micro habits;
  • habit emoji, identity statement, reason, and daily plan;
  • check-in history, streaks, and milestone status;
  • reminder time, snooze settings, theme, and haptic settings;
  • optional reflections or notes you choose to write.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored in local storage on your device. 40 Kun currently does not use user accounts, cloud sync, or an external user database.

Notifications

If you grant permission, the app uses local notifications to remind you about your habits. You can disable notification permission at any time in your phone settings.

Third-party services

The app may be distributed or updated through Google Play, Expo, and operating system services. These services may have their own technical logs and privacy policies. 40 Kun does not add advertising networks or tracking analytics inside the app.

Deleting data

You can clear all local habits and history from the Settings section in the app. Uninstalling the app from your device also removes local app data.

Children’s privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes child-related information has been stored unintentionally, they can delete the app data from the device.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the date on this page will be updated.
