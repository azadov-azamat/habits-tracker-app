Katta o‘zgarish uchun kichik kunlik tizim

A small daily system for meaningful change

40 Kun sizga boshlash, eslash va davom etishga yordam beradi.

40 Kun helps you start, remember, and keep going.

1

Bitta odatga fokus

Focus on one habit

Eng muhim odatni tanlang, uni aniq sabab va kichik kundalik harakatga ulang.

Pick your most important habit and connect it to a clear reason and a small daily action.

2

Mahalliy eslatmalar

Local reminders

Odat vaqti kelganda qurilmangizdagi lokal bildirishnomalar sizni muloyim eslatadi.

Local notifications gently remind you when it is time for your habit.

3

Taraqqiyot ko‘rinadi

Visible progress

Check-inlar, ketma-ketlik va 40 kunlik panjara sizga ritmni yo‘qotmaslikka yordam beradi.

Check-ins, streaks, and a 40-day grid help you keep your rhythm.

Har kun bitta belgi

One mark every day

Quyidagi 40 kunlik panjara ilovadagi tajribaning kichik modeli. Kunlarni bosib ko‘rsangiz, motivatsion jumla ham o‘zgaradi.

This 40-day grid is a tiny model of the app experience. Tap the days and the motivational note changes with your progress.
